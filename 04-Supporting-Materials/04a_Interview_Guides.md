## Pod Leads — Priya & Marcus

**Objective: Understand how work gets prioritized and executed today, identify the operating constraints most likely to emerge as DI scales, diagnose where cross-functional interfaces create friction, and determine where DI Operations can materially improve execution.**

### Start broad: How does the pod operate today?

Can you give me an overview of how your pod operates today—what you're responsible for and how the team gets work done?

From an operating perspective, what works particularly well today?

Where do you see the biggest friction points or inefficiencies?

How well do you think the current model will work as DI grows from 10 to 30? What, if anything, won't scale?

Is there anything that isn't causing pain yet, but you think will if we don't address it soon?

> Probe when something sounds meaningful: Can you give me an example? What's driving that? How frequently does it happen? What's the impact?

### Prioritization & execution

At a high level, how does work get identified, prioritized and ultimately delivered within your pod?

How well does that process work today? Where, if anywhere, does it break down?

How clear are priorities—for you and for the people on your team?

How often do priorities change, and how well does the team absorb those changes?

Where do projects or decisions tend to get stuck?

How clear are ownership and accountability—for work within the pod and for work that crosses pods or functions?

> If prioritization problems emerge, test:  (i) who can request work, (ii) who decides, (iii) how competing requests are resolved, (iv) what happens when something urgent appears, and (v) for examples of things that displaced planned work

> If execution problems emerge: (i) walk through a project where a failure occurred and understand (ii) where it broke, (iii) why, and (iv) who had to intervene

> If ownership problems emerge: (i) ask for an example where ownership was not clear, (ii) what happened, and (iii) whether it was recurring or isolated

### Operating rhythm, information & visibility

How does the team stay aligned on priorities, progress, decisions and issues today?

What regular operating cadence or forums do you have in place, and how well do they work?

Do you have the visibility you need into what your team—and the broader DI organization—is working on?

If I wanted to understand what your pod is working on, why, and how it's progressing, how easy would that be today?

As the organization grows, what about the current operating rhythm or information-sharing model needs to change?

> Watch for clues on current operating model (e.g., weekly cadences, roadmap reviews, project tracking mechanisms, use of tools such as Jira or Notion, reporting mechanisms, etc.)

### Cross-functional coordination

### Within Deerfield Intelligence

How do the two pods work together today? Where does that work well, and where is there friction?

Where do priorities, ownership or dependencies across pods become difficult to manage?

Are there important things that fall between the pods because neither naturally owns them?

As both pods grow, what about cross-pod coordination needs to become more structured?

DI ↔ Broader Deerfield

Which central teams do you depend on most to get your work done?

How well do those interfaces work today? Where is there meaningful friction?

How do you coordinate with those teams today—through regular forums/cadences, named counterparts, or primarily ad hoc?

As DI grows, which of those interfaces most needs to become more structured?

### DI Operations role

What operational work do you or your team spend time on today that you don't think you should be doing?

Where do you find yourself acting as the "glue" because there isn't an obvious owner?

If you had an Operations leader supporting DI, where could that person most materially improve your team's ability to execute?

What would you not want Operations involved in?

### Scaling

As your pod grows, what becomes materially harder to manage?

What works today because the organization is small that won't work at 30?

What operational capability or mechanism do you think DI needs to put in place before it feels necessary?

Conversely, what do you think we should deliberately keep informal or lightweight as we scale?

### Prioritization

Of everything we've discussed, what are the one or two operational issues you think matter most? Why those versus the others? How would you know six months later that we’d actually fixed it?

What would happen if we did nothing about them over the next 12–18 months? What, if anything, would actually break as DI approaches 30 people?

Imagine we're having this conversation 18 months from now and DI has successfully grown from 10 to 30. What operational problem are you most relieved we solved?

If I told you we weren't going to solve that problem, what workaround would you recommend? How long would that workaround remain viable?

Which of the issues we've discussed today do you think others at DI or Deerfield might see differently from you?
