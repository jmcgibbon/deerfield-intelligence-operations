# DI Operations Hub

A lightweight prototype demonstrating how elements of the proposed cross-functional coordination model could be operationalized as Deerfield Intelligence scales.

## Purpose

The prototype is designed to demonstrate a minimum viable coordination layer for recurring operational and cross-functional work.

It provides one front door for requests, translates those requests into structured workflows, makes accountable ownership and cross-functional coordination visible, and surfaces exceptions requiring Operations attention.

## What the Prototype Demonstrates

- One front door for operational and cross-functional requests
- Workflow-based intake and routing
- Clear accountable ownership
- Structured corporate partner involvement
- Lightweight coordination steps
- Visibility into dependencies and blockers
- Exception-based Operations attention
- Human judgment over priority, ownership and escalation

## Design Philosophy

The prototype is intentionally lightweight.

It is not intended to replicate a project-management or enterprise workflow platform. Instead, it demonstrates the minimum operating infrastructure required to make cross-functional work visible and reliable as DI scales from approximately 10 to 30 people.

Automation is applied where the operating rule is clear — such as routing and visibility — while judgment remains with Operations, business owners and specialist functions.

## Relationship to the Assessment

The prototype operationalizes selected concepts described in:

- `01_Operational_Gap_Assessment`
- `02_30-60-90_Day_Operational_Plan`
- `03_Cross-Functional_Coordination_Model`

It should be viewed as an illustrative implementation of the proposed operating model rather than a production-ready system.
